WORK EXPERIENCE / INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT

Industrial Attachment.

During my 10-week internship at EVO Outsourcing Solutions, I supported the EUSS project under NCS, carrying out IT asset management work for Singapore government agencies. I also took on a team leader role, helping coordinate daily tasks, keep the team organised and track progress across assignments.

01 / THE EXPERIENCE

A little about my internship.

As part of EVO Outsourcing Solutions, I worked on the EUSS project under NCS, supporting IT asset and inventory operations for government agencies including GovTech, SNDGO, MND, CPFB, MTI, BCA Academy and PMO.

My responsibilities covered asset tagging, equipment checks, Excel-based tracking and status reporting. I also helped lead the team by keeping assigned work coordinated and organised, and supported HugoSave roadshow activities during the attachment.

02 / TEAM LEADERSHIP

Helping the team stay on track.

Alongside hands-on IT asset work, I had a team leader role during the attachment.

My responsibility was to help keep daily activities organised and make sure assigned work was progressing as expected. This involved coordinating with teammates, following up on tasks and sharing updates when needed.

It gave me experience balancing my own tasks with the wider team's progress, especially while working across different government-agency sites.
